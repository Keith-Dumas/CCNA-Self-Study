# Introduction to Cybersecurity Reflection

## What I Learned

Introduction to Cybersecurity helped me understand the basic principles behind protecting computers, networks, and information.

Before completing this course, I mostly viewed cybersecurity as protecting computers from viruses. This course showed me that cybersecurity is much broader and includes protecting data, managing access, preventing attacks, and educating users.

---

## Key Takeaways

One of the most important concepts I learned was the CIA Triad:

- Confidentiality protects information from unauthorized access.
- Integrity ensures information remains accurate and trustworthy.
- Availability ensures systems remain accessible when needed.

I also learned that many cybersecurity attacks do not rely only on technical weaknesses. Social engineering attacks target people, which means user awareness is a major part of security.

---

## Connection to IT Support

Cybersecurity knowledge is important for IT support because technicians often handle user accounts, devices, and sensitive information.

Understanding security helps IT professionals:
- Recognize suspicious activity
- Assist users with secure practices
- Protect company systems
- Troubleshoot security-related issues

Cybersecurity will be an important skill as I continue developing toward an IT career.
